About

Our holding represents the interests of more than 100 exporters of food products, port infrastructure facilities, farms, transport companies, and companies engaged in maritime shipping. We specialize in the supply of milling wheat, feed corn and barley, soybeans, and rapeseed from Russia, Kazakhstan, and Kyrgyzstan. The supply is carried out in bulk and in big bags. Delivery terms are "door-to-door", CFR, and CIF. Unloading is at the ports of the Caspian Sea. We use a safe form of payment: USDT TRC-20. Payment terms are CAD. You can send a request for the product you need to our e-mail indicated on the website. You will be sent the current price and specification according to the standards of the importing country. We count on mutually beneficial and successful cooperation. The holding team: Caspian Holding Agro
